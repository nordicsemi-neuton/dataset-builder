# Registration and Login

To use the Nordic Edge AI Lab, you must create a free account. Go to the [Nordic Edge Ai Lab](https://ai.lab.nordicsemi.com) to begin.

## Account creation

You can register using one of two methods:

1. **Manual Registration** — Fill in the required fields (first name, last name, company, work email, and password).
2. **Registration with Google** — Click **Continue with Google** and follow the instructions.

Required fields for manual registration:

- First name
- Last name
- Company
- Work email
- Password

_(Screenshot referenced: "Sign-up")_

After submitting the registration form, you will receive an email to verify your address. Check your inbox and follow the verification link to activate your account.

## Login

Once verified, you log in with your credentials through the login interface.

_(Screenshot referenced: "Login")_

### Password recovery

If you forget your password, you can restore access through the password recovery option on the login page, which sends reset instructions to your registered email address.

### Google account users

If you are using Google sign-in but wish to set a regular password for your account, you can do so through the **Forgot password?** option on the login page — without creating a new account. Simply follow the password reset procedure to establish credentials for the existing profile.
